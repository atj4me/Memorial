Privacy Policy — Starbound

Last updated: October 13, 2026

The short version: your words stay yours. Everything you write in Starbound lives in your browser. If you switch on Google Drive sync, a copy is kept in a hidden folder in your own Google Drive. Nothing is ever sold, shared, or shown to anyone else. There is no analytics, no advertising, and no account system.

What Starbound stores

  • On your device (always): your stars (names, dates, dedications, colours, sky positions) and your messages (text, photos, videos, voice notes), kept in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB). This never leaves your device unless you enable sync.
  • In your Google Drive (only if you enable Drive sync): the same data, stored in a hidden application folder that only Starbound can access. It lives in your own Google account — no one else can see it, including the developer.

Google sign-in and permissions

Starbound uses Google sign-in for one purpose only: accessing its hidden folder in your Drive. It requests a single permission — to see, create and delete its own configuration data in your Google Drive (drive.appdata). It cannot see, open, or modify any of your other Drive files.

What is never collected

  • No analytics or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising identifiers.
  • Your content is never transmitted to the developer or any third party.

Deleting your data

  • Destroy a star in the app to delete it and its messages everywhere on the next sync.
  • Sign out of Drive sync in the app to revoke Starbound's access.
  • You can delete Starbound's Drive data at any time through your Google Drive settings.
